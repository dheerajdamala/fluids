ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE • SEARCH THEORY

Autonomous Maze-Solving Robot

Simulate an intelligent 2D kinematic agent executing real-time graph search and heuristic spatial reasoning across complex labyrinths, dynamic obstacles, and dead-end traps.

  • 6 Search Algorithms: A* (Manhattan & Euclidean), BFS, DFS, UCS (Dijkstra), Greedy Best-First
  • Kinematic Agent Engine: Heading beam, dynamic obstacle detection, caterpillar tracks & telemetry antenna
  • Procedural Environments: Recursive Backtracker, Prim's Minimal Spanning Tree, Bottleneck & Spiral
  • Multi-Algorithm Benchmarking: Comparative chart analysis of explored states, frontier limits, and runtimes
FLUID DYNAMICS • FINITE DIFFERENCE CFD

Laminar Pipe Flow Simulation

Numerical solution of water flow through a circular pipe from uniform slug inlet through the hydrodynamic developing entrance region to fully developed Hagen-Poiseuille parabolic profile.

  • Parameterized Velocity: Inlet speed \(U_{in}\) keyed to academic roll number (e.g. 67 m/s)
  • Microtube Geometry: Pipe diameter calibrated to maintain strictly laminar regime (\(Re = 1600 < 2300\))
  • Dual Flow Regimes: Full hydrodynamic entrance length \(L_e\) and fully developed asymptotic zone (\(1.6 L_e\))
  • Scientific Field Maps: 2D velocity colormap, radial profiles \(u(r)\), centerline growth \(u_c(x)\), pressure drop \(\Delta P\)
